Storytelling in Video Games

This page breaks down some of the core aspects of storytelling and discusses how video games are a source of storytelling.

Elements of Storytelling

Storytelling is a form of art and therefore there are various opinions on what is most important or essential to it. Generally speaking, storytelling must contain these elements:

  • Plot
  • Characters
  • Setting
  • Conflict
  • Theme

Plot

The plot is the most obvious element of stories. In simple terms, the plot is what happens throughout the story.

Characters

Characters are the people or creatures who live inside the story. They play key roles in moving the plot forward.

Setting

The setting is where the story takes place. Some forms of storytelling use real places and events, while others are more fictious.

Conflict

The conflict is often the driving force of the plot and what makes the characters interesting. Conflict introduces tension between the characters and/or the enviroment.

Theme

Good stories have themes. These are lessons or central ideas that the story is trying to explore.
