Storytelling in Video Games

This page breaks down some of the core aspects of storytelling and discusses how video games are a source of storytelling.

Elements of Storytelling

Storytelling is a form of art and therefore there are various opinions on what is most important or essential to it. Generally speaking, storytelling must contain these elements:

  • Plot
  • Characters
  • Setting
  • Conflict
  • Theme
