Storytelling in Fire Emblem: Three Houses

What is Fire Emblem: Three Houses?

Fire Emblem: Three Houses is a tactical role-playing game developed by Intelligent Systems and Nintendo. It takes place in a fictional land called Fódlan, in a world mostly inspired by medieval knights. The player takes on the role as a mercenary turned professor at a prestigious school.

Elements of Storytelling in the Game

Below, the elements of storytelling listed on the home page are used to explore how this game specifically tells a story.

Plot

The plot of Fire Emblem: Three Houses changes dramatically depending on which house, or group of students, the player chooses. There are two distinct phases across all of the routes:

  1. The first academic year at the school
  2. The war five years later

The player's choice of house changes which characters and conflicts become the focus of the story. The player experiences the world differently with each perspective.

Characters

The game has around 30 playable characters for the player to bond with. Characters are fully voice-acted and have distinct personalities. While the story focuses on three of them, the other characters still have room to grow and develop. The player spends plenty of time with each character, making it easier to become invested in their personal stories.

Setting

The game takes place on a fictional continent called Fódlan. It is full of medieval fantasy elements such as knights and magic. More specifically, the continent is divided among three factions, each with their own traditions and values. These differences create tension between the characters of each faction and play an essential role in the conflict of the story.

Conflict

The core conflict of the game is the different approaches each character has to better the world. No one character is objectively wrong or right. The player is able to experience different viewpoints by choosing different houses and routes. This makes the conflict more complicated than good against evil.

Themes

These themes are explored through the characters and events of the game. Different routes express these themes in different ways, furthering the emphasis on differing perspectives.

  • Morality of War
  • Burden of trauma and lineage
  • Different Perspectives
